A little about me

Engineering with a practical mindset.

My background in Education Science shaped how I break down complex problems, communicate clearly, and think about the people using a system. I later trained in backend software engineering through ALX Africa and have continued building through hands-on projects and open-source work.

I’m especially interested in the parts of software that are easy to overlook: database guarantees, edge cases, safe retries, tests that expose race conditions, and services that remain understandable as they grow.
